How FoodLodge works

Whether you have food to give or food to find, FoodLodge makes it easy in just a few steps.

1

Sign up & choose your role

Create a free account as a Donor (you have food to share) or a Recipient (you're looking for food).

2

Post or browse listings

Donors post food with a photo and pick-up window. Recipients browse a live map of available food nearby.

3

Claim and collect

Recipients claim a listing and message the donor. The full address is revealed after claiming. Collect within the window.

4

Confirm and rate

Both sides confirm the exchange. Leave a rating. Every completed exchange helps reduce food waste in London.

🍞

For Donors

Have surplus food from your kitchen, restaurant, cafe or event? Post it and let someone in your community benefit.

  • Post a listing in under 2 minutes
  • Set your pick-up window and location
  • Get notified when someone claims
  • Message recipients directly in-app
  • Track your food saved and donations received
  • Enable optional thank-you donations
🙌

For Recipients

Browse available food near you on a live map. Claim what you need and collect for free — no questions asked.

  • Browse a live map of food near you
  • Filter by category (bakery, fruit & veg, etc.)
  • Claim a listing with one tap
  • Full address revealed after claiming
  • Message donors securely in-app
  • Confirm collection and leave a rating
